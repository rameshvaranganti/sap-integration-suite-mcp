# Code of conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully and professionally. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Give constructive feedback focused on the work. Respect privacy and consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks and publication of private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation or ban participants in response to violations, proportionate to the behavior and its impact. These rules apply in project spaces and when representing the project.

Report incidents through a private channel offered by the repository maintainers. If none is available, request a private contact without posting personal information or incident details publicly. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, protect confidentiality and recuse themselves where conflicts exist.
